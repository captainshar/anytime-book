# Review sheet: nurse, physician, pharmacist, or poison center educator

Read [what we are asking](reviewer-brief.md) first. About 30 minutes for the first two pages.

Many of our readers were kept away from doctors as children. Some were taught that seeking care is weakness or sin. They tend to wait too long, and they may not know what an urgent care clinic is. The pages give general information and say so at the top.

## Start here (about 30 minutes)

### 1. Is This an Emergency?

[anytimebook.org/health-and-your-body/is-this-an-emergency](https://anytimebook.org/health-and-your-body/is-this-an-emergency/), section "A simple guide"

- The guide sorts problems into call 911, go to the emergency room, go to urgent care, or wait and call a doctor. It is based on MedlinePlus. Is anything in the wrong group? Is anything missing that kills people who wait (stroke signs, chest pain in women, severe allergic reaction, thoughts of suicide)?
- We say you don't need money, insurance, or ID to be seen for an emergency. That is our plain reading of the federal emergency care law. Is it fair to say?
- We left out first-aid steps (choking, burns, seizures) as too risky to print unchecked. Right call?

### 2. Alcohol, Drugs, and Other Risks

[anytimebook.org/health-and-your-body/alcohol-drugs-and-other-risks](https://anytimebook.org/health-and-your-body/alcohol-drugs-and-other-risks/), the caution box "In an emergency" and the section "Stay safer if you use"

- Overdose signs are given as: won't wake up, very slow breathing, blue or gray lips. Is that list right and complete enough for someone with no training? Is what we tell them to do next right?
- The harm reduction section lists a never-use-alone line and a mail-based naloxone and supplies program. Anything unsafe, or anything a reader needs to know first?

## If you have more time

### 3. Protecting Yourself: first aid and CPR

[anytimebook.org/fun-rest-and-being-you/protecting-yourself](https://anytimebook.org/fun-rest-and-being-you/protecting-yourself/), section "Learn first aid for free"

- We describe Hands-Only CPR as: call 911, then push hard and fast in the center of the chest, for an adult or teen who collapses. Right?

### 4. Keeping a Clean, Safe Home: poison help

[anytimebook.org/a-home-and-daily-life/keeping-a-clean-safe-home](https://anytimebook.org/a-home-and-daily-life/keeping-a-clean-safe-home/)

- We list Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222 but do not say it is open 24 hours, because the official page we read did not say so. Is it? Is "never mix bleach with other cleaners" stated strongly enough?

### 5. Sleep: supplements

[anytimebook.org/first-things/sleep](https://anytimebook.org/first-things/sleep/), "Watch out for"

- We mention melatonin labels and kava, and say to ask a pharmacist before trying a supplement. Is a pharmacist the right first person for a reader with no doctor?
