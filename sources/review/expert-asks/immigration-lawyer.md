# Review sheet: immigration lawyer or accredited representative

Read [what we are asking](reviewer-brief.md) first. This is a short one: about 20 minutes.

Some of our readers are immigrants, or grew up in the US without papers they can reach. The book says very little about immigration on purpose, because we could not check it well enough. We want to know whether the little we say is safe, and whether staying quiet is itself a risk.

### 1. Leaving Family Safely: immigrant survivors

[anytimebook.org/big-life-decisions/leaving-family-safely](https://anytimebook.org/big-life-decisions/leaving-family-safely/), section "Legal protection"

- The page mentions that special protections exist for immigrant survivors of abuse, without naming VAWA self-petitions or T and U visas, and without fees. Is that the right amount to say in 2026? What one sentence would you add, and who should the reader call first?

### 2. Knowing Your Rights and Staying Safe from People Who Prey on You: contacting a federal agency

[anytimebook.org/big-life-decisions/knowing-your-rights](https://anytimebook.org/big-life-decisions/knowing-your-rights/), section "If your boss doesn't pay you right"; also [Staying Safe from People Who Prey on You](https://anytimebook.org/big-life-decisions/staying-safe-from-people-who-prey-on-you/)

- Both pages quote the Department of Labor's statement that it enforces wage law without regard to a worker's immigration status. Given enforcement in 2025 and 2026, should the book add a caution before a reader without status contacts any federal agency? If so, what should it say?

### 3. Food Right Now: SNAP for noncitizens

[anytimebook.org/safety-and-getting-started/food-right-now](https://anytimebook.org/safety-and-getting-started/food-right-now/)

- Eligibility for noncitizens changed in 2025. The page avoids details. Is there a safe, short way to tell mixed-status households whether applying for a citizen child puts anyone at risk, or should we only point to legal help?

### 4. Anything else

- Is there a page where an immigrant reader would be harmed by following advice written for citizens? "Proof That You Exist" (ID and Social Security numbers) is the likeliest: [anytimebook.org/safety-and-getting-started/proof-that-you-exist](https://anytimebook.org/safety-and-getting-started/proof-that-you-exist/).
