# Review sheet: readers who have been there

This one is for people who grew up isolated, homeschooled without oversight, in a high-control group, or otherwise outside mainstream life, and who have built a life since. You are the experts on whether this book would have helped.

You do not have to tell us anything about your own story. We will not ask.

## What to do (30 to 60 minutes)

1. Open [anytimebook.org](https://anytimebook.org).
2. Pick **two or three pages** about things you remember having to figure out alone. Read them the way you would have back then, on your phone if that is how you would have found it.
3. Tell us, for each page:
   - Where did it **talk down** to you?
   - Where did it **scare** you, or make something sound harder than it was?
   - Where was it **vague** about what to actually do next?
   - What did it **get wrong** about what this is really like?
   - What is **missing** that you needed?
4. One more: is there a whole topic the book should have and doesn't?

A few lines per page is plenty. Blunt is good.
