# Review sheet: VITA coordinator, Low Income Taxpayer Clinic, or tax professional

Read [what we are asking](reviewer-brief.md) first. This is one page of the book: about 30 minutes.

Many of our readers are young adults whose parents still claim them as dependents after they have left home, sometimes on purpose. Some have had a parent use their Social Security number. They are often filing a tax return for the first time.

### Your Paycheck and Taxes

[anytimebook.org/money/your-paycheck-and-taxes](https://anytimebook.org/money/your-paycheck-and-taxes/), sections "Protect your tax identity" and "If someone else claimed you or used your number"

Please read these two sections closely. They are built from IRS pages, but we have never watched a real case play out.

- **Who counts as a dependent.** We give the under-19 and student-under-24 rule from the IRS dependents FAQ. Is the short version accurate enough for a reader to tell whether a parent's claim is wrong?
- **When the e-filed return is rejected.** We say that if a return is rejected because the number was already used on another return, the reader can mail a paper return. Is that the right first step? What should they attach or expect next?
- **The CP87B notice.** We say that a reader who gets this notice and truly is not someone's dependent does not need to do anything. Right?
- **Identity Protection PIN.** We only say to get one for next year. We did not say it lets a claimed adult e-file past the reject, because the IRS says that about duplicate dependents and we could not tell whether it covers this case. What is true?
- **Safety.** For some readers, the parent finding out is the danger. Does any step here cause the IRS to contact the parent in a way the reader should know about first?
- **Free filing.** The page lists VITA, Free File, and MyFreeTaxes, and gives no dollar limits because they change each year. It says Direct File is not available right now. Anything out of date for the coming filing season?
- **Missing.** We dropped a section on getting an ITIN because we could not check it well. Should it come back, and what is the safe way to send someone to get one?
