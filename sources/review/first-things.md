## Hand to Gemini

Paste Block A first, then one of these.

### 1. Free beginner reading and math lessons to replace GCFGlobal (1.1, 1.2)

> Topic: Free, self-paced online lessons for adults who are complete beginners at reading (letters, sounds, first words) and at basic math (adding, subtracting, money, fractions). GCFGlobal (edu.gcfglobal.org) now redirects to learnfree.org, and its reading and math lessons appear broken. First, tell me whether LearnFree is still run by the Goodwill Community Foundation and whether its beginning reading and math lessons are available. Then find 3–6 alternatives that are free, made for adults (not children), work on a phone, and need no account or credit card. For each one, say whether it shows ads, has paid upsells, or collects personal data. Exclude TV411, which is retiring in 2026.

### 2. Getting and using a library card with no ID or no fixed address (1.1, and later Areas)

> Topic: How an adult with no photo ID and no permanent address can use a US public library: computers, Wi-Fi, borrowing, and e-books. Find national policy sources (such as the American Library Association and its policies on serving people experiencing homelessness) and examples of large library systems with published no-ID or shelter-address card policies. Do not use Reddit or forums. Say plainly what is usually true nationwide and what varies by library, and give a short script for asking at the desk.

### 3. Adult-friendly hygiene how-to guides, including haircuts and tipping (1.4)

> Topic: Plain-language, step-by-step hygiene guides written for adults (not teens or children) who were never taught: showering, brushing and flossing, deodorant, shaving, menstrual care, and nail care. Also cover how to get a haircut for the first time: booking, what to ask for, and normal US tipping for haircuts. Prefer government health sites (CDC, MedlinePlus, NIDCR, the ADA's MouthHealthy) and established nonprofits. Do not use Reddit or barbershop marketing blogs. Flag any source written mainly for kids or teens.

### 4. Trauma-related sleep help for non-Veterans (1.5)

> Topic: Free, trustworthy help for adults whose sleep is disrupted by trauma, nightmares, or fear at night, who are not Veterans. Include official sources on nightmares after trauma and on treatments such as imagery rehearsal therapy and CBT-I, free CBT-I programs open to the public, and how to find low-cost CBT-I or trauma therapy (health centers, university clinics, sliding-scale directories). Say which resources are open to non-Veterans. Do not include supplements or paid apps.

### 5. Safe, free body-doubling and co-working options (1.3)

> Topic: Free ways for an adult to work on tasks alongside other people ("body doubling"): library quiet study hours, community co-working, online co-working rooms. For each, explain the safety and privacy setup (camera required? real name? moderation? reporting?), because readers may be leaving abusive or controlling situations. Prefer libraries and nonprofits over private apps, and flag any paid upsells.
