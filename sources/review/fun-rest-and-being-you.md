## Hand to Gemini

Paste Block A first, then one of these.

### 10.1 — Free adult classes and "try it once" options

> Topic: Free and very-low-cost places where adults can try a hobby **once, with no commitment and no ID**. Find national programs or national finders for: free library maker spaces and craft or music programs, community college **non-credit** and adult-education enrichment classes (and how to find fee waivers), free museum days, free outdoor programs (for example National Park Service ranger programs, state park "first-timer" programs, and REI-style free clinics that aren't sales pitches), free community choirs or bands, and volunteer-based hobbies (community gardens, animal shelters). For each, say whether ID or proof of address is needed. Include gentle ways to discover what you like if you've never been allowed to choose, and warn about "free class" sales funnels.

### 10.2 — Rest resources that are religion-neutral and current

> Topic: Free, current (2025–2026) writing, audio, and tools that help survivors of high-control religion, abusive homes, or forced labor rest without guilt. They must **not** rely on spiritual or religious framing, and they should come from licensed clinicians or established nonprofits. Include free guided rest or body-scan audio from universities or health systems (for example UCLA Mindful, VA, or NHS), free burnout guides, and plain-language articles on "productivity guilt" and "toxic productivity." For each, note any paid upsell. Flag anything the reader might find triggering for religious-trauma survivors.

### 10.3 — Trauma-informed adult swim lessons, modest swimwear, and women-only swims

> Topic: Adult swimming for very fearful beginners and for people from modest or religious backgrounds. Find: (1) US programs or instructor networks that are explicitly fear-of-water or trauma-informed for adults, with costs; (2) how to find women-only or adults-only swim hours at public pools, and how to ask for them; (3) clear guidance on modest or burkini-style swimwear rules at US public pools and YMCAs; (4) free life-jacket loaner programs besides Sea Tow (state DNR or state parks programs and how to find them). Note costs and any ID or residency requirements, and warn about "drown-proofing" or survival-swim programs that use forced submersion.

### 10.4 — Trauma-informed self-defense for all genders, and how to vet a class

> Topic: Free or low-cost, trauma-informed self-defense and boundary-setting classes in the US that welcome **all genders**, including men who are abuse survivors and people who don't feel safe around police. Find national networks or finders (for example IMPACT programs, Empowerment Self-Defense groups, YWCA programs, and rape-crisis-center workshops) that are currently active in 2026, with cost and who can attend. Give a plain checklist for vetting a self-defense class: signs of fear-based marketing, pressure to buy pepper spray or weapons, long contracts, and touching without consent. Also find free, reputable online boundary-setting or verbal de-escalation lessons.
