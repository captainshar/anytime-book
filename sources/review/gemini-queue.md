## Block A

```text
You are a careful research assistant helping build a free resource guide called *The Anytime Book*. It is for adults who are entering mainstream society from hard or sheltered starting points: childhood neglect or abuse, extreme isolation, abusive or isolating homeschooling, high-control religious groups or cults, or moving from a very different culture. Assume the reader may have **little money, possibly no ID, no family support, and little prior exposure** to this topic. Be trauma-informed, respectful, and never condescending.

**Location:** United States. Go nationwide, not state specific.

**What to find:** The best, most trustworthy resources for the specific topic I give you below. Prioritize in this order: (1) **free**, then (2) **low-cost**. Strongly prefer government programs, public libraries, established nonprofits, and well-known reputable organizations. Include both **national resources** and a clear explanation of **how to find local/state versions**.

**Rules:**

- Only include resources that appear to be **currently active in 2026**. Note if you're unsure.
- **Warn me about common scams or predatory traps** in this topic area that target vulnerable people.
- Do not recommend anything that requires payment to a private company when a free public option exists.
- Flag any resource that requires an ID, money, or another prerequisite to use.

**Output format.** Start with a one-sentence plain-language summary of what this topic is. Then list resources grouped sensibly. For **each resource** give:

- **Name** (with link/phone number)
- **What it offers** (one or two plain sentences)
- **Cost** (free / low-cost / approximate price)
- **Who can use it** (any eligibility rules)
- **How to start** (the first concrete step — a website, a phone number, what to say or click)
- **Why it's trustworthy**

End with two things: a short **"If you only do one thing first"** recommendation, and a short **"Watch out for"** list of traps to avoid. Write everything in **plain, warm, low-reading-level language**, because parts may be shown directly to readers.
```
