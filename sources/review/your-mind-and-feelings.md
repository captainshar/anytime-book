### 1. Faith-keeping recovery from spiritual abuse (3.6)
Topic: Recovery from spiritual abuse for people who want to **keep a faith** but leave a high-control group, church, or leader. ### 3. Homeschool-alumni peer support beyond CRHE (3.1 / 3.6)

Find any other active alumni groups or warmlines, including nonprofits that help alumni with missing records, plus moderated online communities with a real organization behind them. Exclude archived blogs such as Homeschoolers Anonymous (last post 2018). For each, give the official site, current activity (2025–26 dates), cost, and how to join safely.

### 4. Free values and decision-making tools (3.5)
Topic: Free, no-account tools that help adults from controlling backgrounds discover their values and preferences and practice making decisions. Examples include printable values-card sorts, ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) values worksheets from universities or government health services, and free library or community-college workshops. Exclude sites that require an account, sell toolkits, or are run by coaches. Note the reading level of each.

### 5. Free, public somatic and movement resources (3.2)
Topic: Free, gentle, body-based calming videos or audio from universities, hospitals, or government health services that are meant for the public (not employee-only). Examples are chair-based stretching, trauma-informed yoga, and progressive muscle relaxation. Confirm each is open to the public, free, and active in 2026. Avoid anything that promises a cure, uses pain, or sells a course.
