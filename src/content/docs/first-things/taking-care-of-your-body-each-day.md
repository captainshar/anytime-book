## What it is

Daily care — washing, teeth, hair, clean clothes, and dressing for the weather.

## Why it helps you

It keeps you healthy and helps you feel steady around other people.

## Good to have first

Nothing.

## This opens up

Making Friends, Doing Well at Work.

## Where to get help

### Get free soap, toothpaste, and other basics

**[Simply the Basics — Hygiene Locator](https://www.simplythebasics.org/hygienelocator)**
A map that shows places near you that give out free hygiene supplies. That can mean soap, shampoo, toothpaste, and deodorant.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone. Each place sets its own rules.
- **How to start:** Go to the website. Type your city or zip code. Look at the places that come up.
- **Good to know:** The map can be out of date. Call a place before you go, and ask if you need to bring ID.

**[211](https://211.org/)**
A free, private phone line that knows local help in your area. That includes free hygiene supplies, period products, and warm clothes.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Dial 2-1-1 on any phone. You can say, "I need free soap and toothpaste near my zip code." Or search on the website.
- **Good to know:** Hours depend on your area. If you don't have ID, ask for places that don't need it.

### Get free period products and bras

**[Alliance for Period Supplies](https://allianceforperiodsupplies.org/allied-programs/)**
A national group of local programs that give out free pads and tampons.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone who needs period supplies.
- **How to start:** Look for your state on the list. Or call 2-1-1 and ask for "period supplies." Food pantries and faith groups may have them too.
- **Good to know:** Not every state has a program on the list.

**[I Support The Girls](https://isupportthegirls.org/find-a-location/affiliate-network/)**
Gives free bras and period products through local helpers.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** People who need bras or period products.
- **How to start:** Look for a helper ("affiliate") near you on the list. Contact them to ask where they give things out.
- **Good to know:** They can't send items straight to one person. You get them through a local helper or partner place. They are in many states, not all.

### Get your teeth checked

**[Find a Health Center](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov/)**
A government map of community health centers. Many have dentists as well as doctors. They charge less if you earn less.

- **Cost:** Based on your income. They must see you even if you can't pay.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone, with or without insurance.
- **How to start:** Type your city or zip code. Call the closest one and ask, "Do you have a dentist? I don't have much money."
- **Good to know:** Not every health center has a dentist. When you call, ask what papers to bring.

**[Finding Low-Cost Dental Care](https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/health-info/finding-dental-care)**
A government page that lists other ways to get cheaper dental care, like dental school clinics.

- **Cost:** Free to read
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Read the list. Pick one option close to you and call.

### Learn the basics

**[CDC — Hygiene Basics](https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/index.html)**
Short, plain pages on washing your hands, hair, face, feet, and nails, and on period care.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Open the page and pick one topic, like handwashing.

**[National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/)**
The free government weather forecast. It helps you decide what to wear.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Type your city or zip code in the box at the top. Look at the high and low temperature for today.
- **Good to know:** In the US, temperature is in °F. Below 32°F is freezing. Wear layers, a hat, and gloves. Stay out of the cold if you can.

## If you only do one thing first

Each day, try three small things: brush your teeth, wash your face and hands, and put on clean underwear. It takes about five minutes. If you don't have the supplies yet, call 2-1-1 and ask where to get them for free. Your body deserves care, and you can start today.

## Watch out for

- **"Free trial" razors and beauty products.** Ads may offer a free sample if you pay shipping. Often you get signed up for costly monthly charges. Buy basic items at a drugstore or grocery store instead.
- **Medical credit cards at the dentist.** A dental office may offer a card with "no interest" for a while. If you don't pay it all off in time, you can owe interest on the whole bill. Ask about a payment plan or a health center first. The [CFPB explains medical credit cards](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-should-i-know-about-medical-credit-cards-and-payment-plans-for-medical-bills-en-1827/).
- **Paying more for pink.** Razors, shampoo, and deodorant made "for women" often cost more than the same thing "for men." Compare prices and buy the cheaper one. It works the same.
