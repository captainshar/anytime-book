This Area covers the parts of life that aren't work, chores, or getting by. That means finding things you like, resting, being around water, and knowing what to do when someone gets hurt. If your time was never your own, or fun and rest were treated as wrong, you may be starting these as an adult. They can be learned like anything else in this book.

Nothing here is urgent. If you aren't safe, or aren't eating, start with [Safety and Getting Started](/safety-and-getting-started/). Otherwise, start with whichever page is closest to something you've wanted.

- [10.1 Finding What You Enjoy](/fun-rest-and-being-you/finding-what-you-enjoy/): how to find out what you like, and where to try things for free.
- [10.2 Resting Without Guilt](/fun-rest-and-being-you/resting-without-guilt/): what counts as rest, why stopping can feel wrong, and how to practice it.
- [10.3 Swimming and Water Safety](/fun-rest-and-being-you/swimming-and-water-safety/): how to stay safe near water now, and how to find an adult beginner lesson.
- [10.4 Protecting Yourself](/fun-rest-and-being-you/protecting-yourself/): what to do when someone is badly hurt, and what a good self-defense class teaches.
