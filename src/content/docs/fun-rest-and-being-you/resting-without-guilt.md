## What it is

Learning that rest is allowed — that you’re allowed to exist without always being useful.

## Why it helps you

Many survivors were only valued for what they produced. Rest heals your body and your worth. You don’t have to earn it.

## This opens up

Sleep, calm, and a kinder life.

## Where to get help

If sitting still makes you feel panicky or guilty, that is common. It is not laziness. Your body learned to stay busy to stay safe. It can learn that rest is safe, too. These free tools can help.

### Understand why rest feels hard

**[The 4Fs: A Trauma Typology (Pete Walker)](https://www.pete-walker.com/fourFs_TraumaTypologyComplexPTSD.htm)**
A free article by a therapist who writes about growing up in hard homes. It explains why some people can't stop working ("flight") or can't stop pleasing others ("fawn").

- **Cost:** Free to read
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with internet
- **How to start:** Open the link and read slowly. Take breaks if it brings up big feelings.
- **Good to know:** The site also sells his books. You don't need to buy anything to read the articles.

**[Burnout: signs, causes, and how to recover (Mental Health America)](https://mhanational.org/resources/burnout-signs-causes-recover/)**
A free, plain-language page from a long-running mental health nonprofit. It explains burnout and gives small steps, like short breaks, that work even when you are busy.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with internet
- **How to start:** Read the part on how to recover. Pick one small step to try today.
- **Good to know:** It also explains how burnout is different from depression.

### Be kind to yourself

**[Self-Compassion Break (Self-Compassion.org)](https://self-compassion.org/exercises/exercise-2-self-compassion-break/)**
A short exercise for when a harsh inner voice tells you that you're lazy. It teaches you to talk to yourself the way a kind friend would. The site is run by Dr. Kristin Neff, a researcher on self-kindness.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with internet
- **How to start:** Open the link and follow the steps. It takes a few minutes.
- **Good to know:** The site has more free written and audio practices. It also sells books and courses. You don't need them.

### Read about rest (free from the library)

**[Libby, the library app](https://libbyapp.com/)**
A free app that lets you borrow ebooks and audiobooks from your public library. Books go back on their own, so there are no late fees.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with a library card. Many US libraries let you get a digital card in the app with just your phone number.
- **How to start:** Download Libby. Search for your library. If you don't have a card, tap "I Would Like A Card," then "Use My Phone Number."
- **Good to know:** Needs a phone or computer. Popular books may have a waitlist. You can put your name on it.

Two books many people find helpful. Look for them in Libby or at your library:

- ***[Rest Is Resistance](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/tricia-hersey/rest-is-resistance/9780316365536/)* by Tricia Hersey.** Says rest is a basic human right, not a reward. The author founded The Nap Ministry.
- ***[Laziness Does Not Exist](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Laziness-Does-Not-Exist/Devon-Price/9781982140113)* by Devon Price.** A psychologist explains that what looks like "lazy" is usually tiredness, fear, or not knowing the first step.

### Check in with yourself

**[Mental Health America screening tools](https://screening.mhanational.org/)**
Short online quizzes for things like anxiety, depression, and trauma (PTSD). At the end, you get your results in plain words and ideas for next steps.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone
- **How to start:** Click "Take a Mental Health Test." Pick the one that fits how you feel.
- **Good to know:** A quiz is not a diagnosis. It can help you decide whether to talk to someone. You don't need to make an account.

**[Insomnia Coach (VA app)](https://mobile.va.gov/app/insomnia-coach)**
A free sleep app from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. It is made for everyone, not only veterans. It teaches simple, proven ways to sleep better.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with a smartphone
- **How to start:** Download it from the Apple or Google app store. Search "Insomnia Coach."
- **Good to know:** If you have sleep apnea, seizures, bipolar disorder, or sleepwalking, talk to a doctor before changing your sleep habits.

### If rest brings up something too big

**[988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/)**
Free, private support from a trained counselor, any time of day or night. You don't have to be thinking of suicide. You can reach out if you just feel overwhelmed.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone in the US
- **How to start:** Call or text [988](tel:988), or chat at [chat.988lifeline.org](https://chat.988lifeline.org/).
- **Good to know:** Open 24/7. Text and chat are also available in Spanish.

## If you only do one thing first

Set a timer for five minutes. Sit or lie somewhere comfortable and do nothing useful. If guilt shows up, you can say to yourself: "I'm allowed to rest. I'm safe right now." Then let the timer run out. That's it. You just practiced rest.

## Watch out for

- **Paid "nervous system" coaches.** Some online coaches charge a lot to "fix" burnout or trauma, but have no license. Start with free tools, and look for licensed professionals if you want more help.
- **Groups that sell peace.** If a group says you can only find rest by buying their program, cutting off friends, or obeying a leader, walk away. That is control dressed up as wellness.
- **Pills and teas that "cure" exhaustion.** Supplements sold online to fix burnout or "adrenal fatigue" are not a cure. Rest, help, and fewer demands are what help.
- **Quizzes that charge at the end.** Some sites ask personal questions and then want payment to show your results. Use free sites like Mental Health America instead.
- **Telling yourself you'll rest "after."** There will always be one more thing to do. Rest doesn't have to wait until everything is done.
