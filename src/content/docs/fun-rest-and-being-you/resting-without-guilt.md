## What it is

Learning that rest is allowed — that you’re allowed to exist without always being useful.

## Why it helps you

Many survivors were only valued for what they produced. Rest heals your body and your worth. You don’t have to earn it.

## This opens up

Sleep, calm, and a kinder life.
