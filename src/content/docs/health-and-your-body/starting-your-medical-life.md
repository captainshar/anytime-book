## What it is

Building a health record from nothing — finding a doctor, catching up on shots, and getting basic care.

## Why it helps you

If you’ve never had regular care, this is the first step. Free and low-cost clinics exist for exactly this.

## Good to have first

Proof That You Exist (helpful, not always required).

## This opens up

Health Insurance, ongoing care.
