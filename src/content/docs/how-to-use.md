The book has 10 **Areas**, like Money or Health and Your Body. Each Area has a handful of **Topics**. Each Topic is one page about one thing you can learn or get, like a bank account or a doctor.

Read in any order. Some Topics list others that are "good to have first," but those are suggestions, not requirements.

## What’s on every Topic page

Every Topic page has the same parts, in the same order, so you can skip to the one you need.

- **What it is:** one or two sentences on what the Topic covers.
- **Why it helps you:** what changes in your life once you have it.
- **Good to have first:** other Topics that make this one easier.
- **Start here:** the one step to take first.
- **How it works:** the steps, on Topics that are a skill you do yourself, like budgeting or cooking.
- **Where to get help:** free and low-cost services. Each one lists the cost, who can use it, how to start, and what to know before you go.
- **Watch out for:** scams and traps, how to spot them, and what to do instead.
- **This opens up:** Topics that get easier once you have this one.

## Things that are true across the book

- **You don’t need money, ID, or family to start.** [Safety and Getting Started](/safety-and-getting-started/) covers getting ID, a safe place to sleep, and food first.
- **Most of the help is free.** When something costs money, the page says how much, and lists free options where they exist.
- **Every resource was checked on its own official website.** Phone numbers and programs still change. If one is wrong, use the **Edit page** link at the bottom of the page to tell me.
- **Some help is run by religious groups.** When it is, the page says so, and says whether you have to take part in anything religious. If the group doesn’t say, the page tells you to ask.
- **On a phone, you can tap a phone number to call it.**
