This book is a **map** to everyday adult life in the United States: ID, money, housing, health, work, friends, and more. It's free. Read the parts you need, in any order.

It's for adults who didn't get taught these things growing up. That includes people who were homeschooled, kept apart from others, raised in a strict group, neglected, or who moved here from another country. It's not your fault you didn't learn this earlier. People learn it at every age, which is why this is called the **Anytime Book**.

- **If you're in danger right now,** call [911](tel:911).
- **If you have no safe bed, no ID, or no food,** start with [Safety and Getting Started](/safety-and-getting-started/).
- **Otherwise,** pick the Area below that's giving you the most trouble.

Each Topic explains what it is, why it's worth having, and where to get free or low-cost help. Take what helps. Leave the rest. Come back any time.

This is the **United States Edition**: the programs, phone numbers, and laws in it are for the US. [Live somewhere else? Help make your country's edition.](/how-this-book-was-made/)

Read the sections using the menu on the left (on a phone, tap the menu button at the top) or the Skill Areas below. Or read the same book offline: [download the free ebook (epub)](https://github.com/captainshar/anytime-book/releases/latest/download/anytime-book.epub).
