This book is a **map** for joining mainstream society in the United States, 2026. Read all of it or just the parts you need, in any order.

A lot of people grow up without someone who teaches them how life in mainstream society works. For example, people who were home-schooled, kept apart from others, raised in a strict group, neglected, or who moved here from another country might need to learn the skills in this book. It's not your fault that you didn't get the chance to learn these skills earlier. Adults learn them every day. So can you. That's why this resource is called the **Anytime Book**.

Take what helps. Leave the rest. Come back any time.

This is the **United States Edition**: the programs, phone numbers, and laws in it are for the US. [Live somewhere else? Help make your country's edition.](/how-this-book-was-made/)
