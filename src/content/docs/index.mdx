This book is a **map** for joining mainstream society, United States edition. Read all of it or some of it, in any order.

Maybe no one taught you the things in here. That is not your fault. A lot of people reach adulthood without these skills — people who were home-schooled, kept apart from others, raised in a strict group, or who came from another country or another world. The skills can still be learned. People learn them every day. So can you.

Take what helps. Leave the rest. Come back any time.
