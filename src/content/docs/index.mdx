The Anytime Book is a free guide to the practical parts of adult life in the United States. It covers getting ID, finding a safe place to sleep, handling money, seeing a doctor, finding work, and making friends. Each page explains what something is, why it’s worth having, and where to get free or low-cost help.

## Who this is for

Adults who missed some of what most people pick up growing up. That includes people who were:

- kept out of school, or homeschooled with little real schooling
- raised in a strict religious or high-control group
- neglected, isolated, or cut off from the outside world
- new to the US, or new to mainstream American life

You might be 18 or 58. You might be leaving right now, or you left years ago and are still filling in gaps. Not knowing these things says something about how you were raised. It says nothing about what you can learn.

## Where to start

You don’t have to read in order.

- **If you’re in danger right now,** call [9-1-1](tel:911).
- **If you have no ID, no safe bed, or no food,** start with [Safety and Getting Started](/safety-and-getting-started/).
- **If you’re safe for now,** pick the Area that’s causing you the most trouble.
- **If you’re not sure,** read [How to use this book](/how-to-use/). It takes about two minutes.

## What this book is not

- **Not legal, medical, or financial advice.** It points you to people who can give you that.
- **Not a crisis line.** If you’re thinking about ending your life, call or text [988](tel:988), any time.
- **Not for other countries, yet.** This is the United States Edition: the programs, phone numbers, and laws are American. [Help make your country’s edition.](/how-this-book-was-made/)
