Parts of this book were gathered with help from AI. AI is a computer tool that can read a huge amount of writing very fast and sum it up. I want to tell you that plainly, and tell you why.

## Why I used it

This book covers a lot: money, health, a home, school, work, your rights, and more. Finding good, free help for every Topic would take one person years. AI helped me do the first round of searching in weeks. That means you get this map sooner.

## How I keep it honest

AI is fast, but it makes mistakes. When I checked its first notes, about 1 in 4 details were wrong — like an old phone number, or a rule that had changed.

So AI is never the last word here. A person checks every phone number, website, and rule against the group’s own website before it goes in this book. Until a Topic has been checked, it says **Coming soon**.

## A thought about new things

A lot of us grew up around people who were afraid of new things — new ideas, new places, new tools, the outside world. Sometimes that fear kept us from things that would have helped us.

That kind of worry isn’t only found in strict families or small groups. The whole world gets nervous about new tools. People once worried about trains, telephones, and the internet, too. Being careful is smart. If AI makes you uneasy, that makes sense, and you never have to use it.

For me, trying new tools is part of joining the world. I check their work, and I keep what helps. I want to use every good tool I can to help us move forward.
