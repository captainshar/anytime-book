## What it is

Simple ways to settle your body when fear or stress takes over.

## Why it helps you

When your body feels safe, you can think, learn, and rest. These tools are free and you carry them everywhere.

## Good to have first

A Safe Place to Sleep.

## This opens up

Clear thinking for every other Topic.

## Where to get help

:::caution[If you are in crisis]
Call or text **988** any time, day or night. It is free. If you are in danger right now, call **911**.
:::

### Things you can do right now, with nothing

You do not need a phone or money for these. Try one and notice how your body feels.

- **Name what you sense.** Look around. Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste.
- **Feel your feet.** Press your feet into the floor. Notice the floor holding you up.
- **Breathe out longer than you breathe in.** Breathe in for a count of 4. Breathe out slowly for a count of 6. Do this a few times.
- **Use water.** Run cold or warm water over your hands. Notice how it feels.
- **Say where you are.** "My name is ___. Today is ___. I am in ___. I am safe right now."

If one of these makes you feel worse, stop. Try a different one. There is no wrong way.

### Free apps

**[PTSD Coach](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/appvid/mobile/ptsdcoach_app.asp)**
A free app from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). It has quick tools for when fear, panic, or bad memories take over. You do not need to be a veteran or have a diagnosis.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Get "PTSD Coach" from your phone's app store. No phone? Use [PTSD Coach Online](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/apps/ptsdcoachonline/) on any computer, like one at a library.
- **Good to know:** The online version needs no login.

**[iChill](https://www.traumaresourceinstitute.com/ichill)**
A free app that teaches six simple skills to help your body settle. It comes from the Trauma Resource Institute, a nonprofit.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Kids, teens, and adults.
- **How to start:** Get "iChill" from your app store, or use the web version linked on that page.
- **Good to know:** Comes in English, Spanish, Arabic, Hebrew, and Ukrainian.

**[Virtual Hope Box](https://mobile.health.mil/Apps/Native-Apps/Virtual-Hope-Box)**
A free app where you keep things that comfort you, like photos, songs, and kind words. It also has calming exercises and simple games to quiet a racing mind.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Get "Virtual Hope Box" from your app store. Add one photo or song that makes you feel safe.
- **Good to know:** Made by the U.S. Defense Health Agency.

**[Healthy Minds Program](https://humin.org/)**
A free app with short lessons and practices. Some you can do while walking or doing chores, if sitting still is hard for you.

- **Cost:** Free. Run by a nonprofit.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Get "Healthy Minds Program" from your app store.
- **Good to know:** It may ask you to make an account.

### Free recordings and videos

**[UCLA Mindful free guided meditations](https://www.uclahealth.org/programs/marc/free-guided-meditations/guided-meditations)**
Short, calm recordings from UCLA Health. Some are only 3 to 5 minutes long.

- **Cost:** Free
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Press play on a short breathing meditation or body scan. No login needed.
- **Good to know:** Comes in about 20 languages, including Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, and American Sign Language.

**[Therapy in a Nutshell](https://www.youtube.com/@TherapyinaNutshell)**
Free YouTube videos by Emma McAdam, a licensed marriage and family therapist. She explains what fear and "freeze" do to your body, and gives small steps to feel better.

- **Cost:** Free on YouTube. She also sells courses. You do not need them.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone with internet.
- **How to start:** Search her channel for "freeze response."
- **Good to know:** YouTube may show ads first.

## If you only do one thing first

Put PTSD Coach on your phone today, while you feel okay. Then it is there when a hard moment comes. Or, right now, try breathing out a little longer than you breathe in. Your body can learn that it is safe, one breath at a time.

## Watch out for

- **People who message you offering a "nervous system reset."** Real therapists do not send surprise private messages selling help. Do not pay them.
- **Promises of a "cure" or a "99% success rate."** Calming your body takes practice. No course can promise a cure.
- **Anything that hurts.** These skills should feel gentle. If a teacher pushes you through pain or panic, you can stop.
- **Paying for basic breathing tools.** The best tools for this are free. You do not need a paid subscription.
