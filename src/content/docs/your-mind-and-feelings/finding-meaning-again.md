## What it is

Rebuilding what you believe and value after leaving a strict group or faith — including how to handle holidays, right and wrong, and the grief of what you left.

## Why it helps you

Leaving can take your whole map of the world with it. You get to build a new one, on your own terms, at your own pace.

## Good to have first

Naming What Happened.

## This opens up

A steadier sense of who you are.
