## What it is

Finding your own likes, opinions, and choices — and learning how to *decide* things for yourself.

## Why it helps you

If others always chose for you, choosing can feel scary or even impossible. It’s a skill, and it grows. Your taste and your “yes” and “no” are yours to find.

## Good to have first

Being Kind to Yourself.

## This opens up

Finding What You Enjoy, every big decision ahead.
