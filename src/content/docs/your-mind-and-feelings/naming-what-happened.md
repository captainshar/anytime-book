## What it is

Learning the words for what you went through — like neglect, or emotional, spiritual, or educational abuse, or C-PTSD.

## Why it helps you

Having a name for it is a huge relief. It shows you that you are not broken, and that you are not alone.

## This opens up

Being Kind to Yourself, Calming Your Body.

## Where to get help

:::caution[If you are in danger or thinking about ending your life]
Call or text **988** any time, day or night. It is free. You can also chat at [chat.988lifeline.org](https://chat.988lifeline.org/). If you are in danger right now, call **911**.
:::

### Learn the words

**[Pete Walker's free articles on Complex PTSD](https://www.pete-walker.com/)**
Pete Walker is a licensed therapist who writes about growing up with abuse or neglect. His short articles explain things like "emotional flashbacks" and the harsh "inner critic" in plain words.

- **Cost:** Free to read online.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Read ["13 Steps for Managing Flashbacks"](https://www.pete-walker.com/13StepsManageFlashbacks.htm).
- **Good to know:** Needs internet. His book, *Complex PTSD: From Surviving to Thriving*, is in many libraries. [Find it at a library near you](https://search.worldcat.org/search?q=Complex+PTSD+From+Surviving+to+Thriving+Walker).

**[The Laundry List, from Adult Children of Alcoholics & Dysfunctional Families (ACA)](https://adultchildren.org/laundry-list/)**
A short list of 14 traits that many people share after growing up in a hard home. You do not need to have had a parent who drank. Many readers say, "This is me."

- **Cost:** Free.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone who grew up in a home with chaos, neglect, or abuse.
- **How to start:** Read the list. If it fits, ACA has free meetings too (see below).
- **Good to know:** Needs internet to read online.

### Talk with people who get it

**[Out of the Storm](https://www.outofthestorm.website/)**
A free online forum for adults living with the effects of long-term abuse or neglect. You do not need a diagnosis to join.

- **Cost:** Free.
- **Who can use it:** English-speaking adults.
- **How to start:** Read the "Symptoms" page first. If you want to post, make a username. You do not have to use your real name.
- **Good to know:** This is peer support, not a crisis line.

**[ACA meetings](https://adultchildren.org/meeting-search/)**
Free group meetings for adults who grew up in troubled homes. There are meetings in person, online, and by phone.

- **Cost:** Free. You can give money, but you never have to.
- **Who can use it:** Any adult from a troubled home.
- **How to start:** Use the meeting search. Pick an online or phone meeting. You can just listen.
- **Good to know:** ACA is a 12-step group. It uses words like "Higher Power." You get to decide what that means for you, or skip it.

### If you were homeschooled

**[Coalition for Responsible Home Education (CRHE) — help for homeschool alumni](https://crhe.org/guides/a-message-for-homeschool-alumni/)**
A group started by people who were homeschooled. It has free guides on healing, getting a diploma or transcript, and what to do if your schooling left gaps. It also runs online groups just for homeschool alumni.

- **Cost:** Free.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone who was homeschooled.
- **How to start:** Read ["How to Pursue Healing and Justice"](https://crhe.org/guides/a-message-for-homeschool-alumni/pursue-healing-and-justice/). To join an alumni group, sign up on the [alumni groups page](https://crhe.org/homeschool-alumni-groups/).
- **Good to know:** Groups are online and led by trained alumni. You can email info@responsiblehomeschooling.org or call [617-765-7096](tel:16177657096).

### If what happened included sexual abuse

**[RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline](https://rainn.org/)**
Free, private support for anyone hurt by sexual abuse or assault. That includes abuse that happened when you were a child.

- **Cost:** Free.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone, any age.
- **How to start:** Call [1-800-656-4673](tel:18006564673), text HOPE to 64673, or chat at [rainn.org/hotline](https://rainn.org/hotline).
- **Good to know:** Open 24/7, in English and Spanish.

### You are not alone

**[*Educated*, by Tara Westover](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/550168/educated-by-tara-westover/)**
A true story by a woman who was kept out of school and later left her family to learn. Many former homeschoolers say it helped them feel less alone.

- **Cost:** Free to borrow from a library.
- **Who can use it:** Anyone.
- **How to start:** Ask your library for the book or audiobook. It also comes in Spanish (*Una educación*).
- **Good to know:** Some parts describe injury and abuse. Go slowly, and stop when you need to.

## If you only do one thing first

Read the ACA Laundry List. It takes five minutes. If parts of it sound like you, that is not a sign you are broken. It is a sign that what happened to you was real, and that many people share it.

## Watch out for

- **Unlicensed "trauma coaches."** Anyone can call themselves a coach. They have no license and no one checks their work. Look for a license like LCSW, LPC, LMFT, or psychologist, and check it with your state.
- **Big fees for "healing packages."** Be careful of anyone who wants hundreds or thousands of dollars up front for a course or retreat. Free groups and library books are a safe place to start.
- **Pushing you to dig up everything fast.** Good help goes at your pace. If someone pushes you to relive the worst parts before you feel safe, you can stop.
- **"You're not ready" or "your energy is off."** If a helper blames you when things don't get better, that is a red flag. You are allowed to leave.
- **Online "tests" that diagnose you.** Quizzes can help you think. Only a licensed professional can diagnose something like C-PTSD.
